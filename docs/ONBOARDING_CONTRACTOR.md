# Contractor Onboarding Guide

Getting started with HomeGentic as a licensed contractor.

---

## 1. Create Your Account

1. Go to `homegentic.app` and click **Get Started**.
2. Sign in with **Internet Identity** — passkey-based, no password required.
3. Select **Contractor** as your role.
4. You start on **ContractorFree** — free forever for quoting and accepting jobs, with a 3% referral fee ($20 minimum) on the awarded value of each winning bid.

---

## 2. Complete Your Profile

A complete profile earns more quote invitations and homeowner trust.

1. Go to **Settings → Profile**.
2. Fill in:
   - Business name and bio
   - **License number** (displayed publicly on your profile)
   - Service area and ZIP codes
   - Specialties (HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical, Roofing, etc.)
   - Profile photo
3. Save — your public profile is now live at `/contractors/<your-id>`.

> Your license number is displayed on your public profile and on every co-signed job record, building verifiable trust with homeowners.

---

## 3. Receive and Respond to Quote Requests

Homeowners post quote requests for specific jobs. You'll be notified when one matches your service area and specialties.

1. Open **Quotes** from the sidebar.
2. Review the job description, property address, and requested timeline.
3. Click **Submit Bid** to respond with your price and estimated start date.
4. The homeowner selects a contractor — you'll be notified if chosen.

**ContractorFree** allows unlimited quote requests. **ContractorPro** ($40/mo) adds 50 photos per job and priority placement, and drops the 3% referral fee.

---

## 4. Co-Sign Completed Jobs

After completing work, the homeowner will send you a co-sign request. Your signature creates a dual-verified, blockchain-anchored record — the most credible form of maintenance documentation.

1. You'll receive an email or in-app notification with a co-sign link.
2. Review the job details (description, date, cost, photos).
3. Click **Co-Sign Job** to add your verified contractor signature.

> Co-signed records are tied to your principal and license number on the ICP blockchain — they cannot be altered retroactively.

---

## 5. Build Your Trust Score

HomeGentic's trust score is derived from:

- Number of co-signed completed jobs
- Homeowner reviews (rate-limited to 10/day/reviewer to prevent manipulation)
- Profile completeness
- License number on file

A higher trust score increases your ranking in homeowner search results.

---

## 6. Upgrade to ContractorPro

| Feature | ContractorFree | ContractorPro |
|---|---|---|
| Quote requests | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Photos per job | 5 | 50 |
| Profile placement | Standard | Priority |
| Referral fee | 3% per winning bid, $20 min | None |
| Price | Free | $40 / mo |

Upgrade from **Settings → Subscription**.

---

## 7. Recurring Service Contracts

For repeat clients (HVAC tune-ups, pest control, landscaping):

1. Go to **Recurring Services → New Contract**.
2. Set the service type, frequency, and property.
3. Log each visit as it occurs — the homeowner's record stays up to date automatically.

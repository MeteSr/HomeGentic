# Homeowner Onboarding Guide

Getting started with HomeGentic — your blockchain-verified home maintenance record.

---

## 1. Create Your Account

1. Go to `homegentic.app` and click **Get Started**.
2. Sign in with **Internet Identity** — a passkey-based, passwordless system built on ICP.
3. On first sign-in you'll be prompted to choose your role: select **Homeowner**.
4. You start on the **Free** tier, which lets you explore the app before subscribing.

---

## 2. Add Your First Property

1. From the Dashboard, click **Add Property**.
2. Enter your address, property type, year built, and square footage.
3. Click **Save** — your property is now registered on the ICP blockchain.

> **Tip:** You can add rooms and fixtures to a property to track maintenance at the system level (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roof, etc.).

---

## 3. Verify Your Property

Verification unlocks features like Insurance Reports and Report sharing.

| Level | How | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| **Basic** | Upload a recent utility bill | Insurance Risk Report, Share Report |
| **Premium** | Upload a deed or county tax record | All Basic features + premium badge |

1. Open your property and click **Verify Property**.
2. Select your verification level and upload the required document.
3. An admin reviews it — Basic verification typically completes within 1 business day.

---

## 4. Log Maintenance Jobs

Every repair, inspection, or upgrade gets its own job record.

1. Open your property → **Add Job**.
2. Fill in the description, date, cost, and category.
3. **DIY jobs** can be signed by you alone.
4. **Contractor jobs** require the contractor to co-sign — send them an invite link.
5. Dual-signed records carry the highest credibility for buyers and insurers.

---

## 5. Get AI-Powered Insights

- **Voice Agent** — tap the mic icon from your dashboard and ask anything: "What maintenance is due this fall?" or "How do I winterize my pipes?"
- **Predictive Maintenance** — the app generates seasonal task reminders based on your home's age and systems.
- **Market Intelligence** — see ROI-ranked renovation projects for your market.

---

## 6. Share Your Record

When selling or refinancing, share your verified maintenance history:

1. Open your property → **Reports ▾** → **Share Report**.
2. Choose a visibility level (summary or full detail) and an expiry window.
3. Copy the link and send it to your buyer, agent, or lender.

Insurance carriers can receive a risk report via **Reports ▾** → **Insurance Report**.
